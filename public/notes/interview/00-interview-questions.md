## Tips for Interview Success

| Tip | Description |
|-----|-------------|
| **Be Authentic** | Don't memorize answers word by word — understand them and speak naturally |
| **STAR Method** | Use Situation, Task, Action, Result for behavioral questions |
| **Ask Questions** | Always have 2-3 thoughtful questions ready for the interviewer |
| **Body Language** | Maintain eye contact, sit upright, and smile genuinely |
| **Research** | Know about the company before the interview |
| **Practice** | Practice with a friend or in front of a mirror |
